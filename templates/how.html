HOW TO USE

1

Position Your Camera

Ensure your webcam is clear and pointed toward the road or traffic sign images you wish to identify.

2

Launch Detection

Press the START DETECTION button on the home screen to open the live YOLOv11 processing feed.

3

Monitor Results

The system will highlight detected signs with a bounding box. Recognition details and reference images will appear in the right-hand panel.
